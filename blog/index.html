What happened?

It wasn't me

The blog worked and looked fine on paper, but it didn't feel like my site. I'd rather have nothing than something that doesn't fit.

A rethink, not a repair

Patching it wouldn't fix the real problem, so the plan is to start fresh with a simpler idea and a better flow for writing.

Writing will be back
